Title: Biometrics
Description: Learn when and how to provide biometrics for Australian visa applications, what we use them for, and where to provide them.
Under the Migration Act 1958 and the Migration Regulations 1994, you may be required to provide personal identifiers as part of the visa application process. ‘Personal identifiers’ include your fingerprints and an image of your face. These features of your body, which can be used to identify you, are also known as ‘biometrics’. We will tell you if you need to provide biometrics, and which biometrics you need to provide.
What we use biometrics for
We use your biometrics to identify you. This helps to: protect you from identity fraud make travel to Australia safer secure our borders. We store the biometrics you give us. When you enter Australia, or apply for another visa, we might take your biometrics again and compare them to your stored biometrics to confirm your identity. We collect, store and use your biometrics in line with the requirements of the Migration Act and the Privacy Act.
Who we check biometrics with
We may check your biometrics with other Australian or international agencies to verify: your identity your criminal history your immigration history. Important information about the collection, use and disclosure of your personal identifying information, including biometrics, is contained in our Privacy Policy, Form 1442i Privacy Notice and Form 1243i Your personal identifying information.
How we store biometrics
We store your images and fingerprints in a secure database. Only authorised officers of the department can access the database.
How to provide your biometrics
If you are outside Australia We will tell you if you need to attend an Australian Biometrics Collection Centre (ABCC) in person or if you are eligible to use the Australian Immi App to provide your biometrics. VFS Global is contracted by the department to operate ABCCs in a number of countries (see list of countries below). You must make an appointment to provide your biometrics at an ABCC (see VFS Global). You may need to travel to another country to attend an ABCC. ABCCs charge a service fee for biometrics collection. VFS Global does not answer questions about visa applications. Make sure you bring your valid travel document, such as the passport linked to your visa application, to your appointment, otherwise VFS will generally not be able to collect your biometrics. If you are within Australia Collection for onshore applications is by appointment only. We will send you a letter or invitation to attend an appointment in-person. Bring the appointment letter and your original passport or other valid travel document to your appointment. Accepted travel documents include: a valid passport Certificate of Identity ImmiCard UNHCR Identity Card Convention Travel Document (also known as Titre de Voyage). If you applied offshore for a visa which requires biometrics, and are now in Australia, you must tell the relevant office in the country where you applied, informing them that you are now onshore. You may need to travel offshore to an ABCC to provide your biometrics. If you need to contact the overseas post, contact us through our Global Service Centre (GSC).
At your biometrics collection appointment
Staff collecting your biometrics will: take an image of your face with a digital camera scan all 10 of your fingertips with a digital finger scanner. Your face must be clearly visible from the bottom of your chin to above your eyebrows, including both cheeks and both ears. If you wear glasses or a religious head covering, you might have to remove them. If you do, you can have your biometrics collected: in private by someone of the same gender. If you have temporary injuries to your face or fingerprints, wait until they have healed before providing your biometrics. Alternatively, make a written request for more time, giving the reasons, and upload the request to your ImmiAccount. If you are missing a finger, ABCC staff will collect fingerprints of your remaining fingers. If you have a mehndi or other temporary decoration of your hands or fingers, contact VFS Global to find out if you can still give fingerprints. You must have a parent or guardian with you if you are: a minor under the laws of the country where the biometrics are collected incapable of understanding what biometrics are for.
