Department Overview
The Department of Education Support Services (ESS) is one of the professional departments of the Federal Ministry of Education, established in 1988 in line with the recommendations of the Dotun Phillips Civil Service Reforms, and restored in 2013 by the Office of the Head of the Civil Service of the Federation. The Department provides strategic professional support services that strengthen the quality, relevance, and effectiveness of education in Nigeria through policy implementation, technical expertise, stakeholder collaboration, and institutional support.
The Department coordinates programmes and initiatives in teacher support and development, evaluation and accreditation of educational qualifications, guidance and counselling, school health and sports education, instructional materials development, examination ethics and campus safety, and national and international educational cooperation. It also facilitates bilateral and multilateral education partnerships, promotes collaboration with development partners, and supports the implementation of national education priorities through evidence-based interventions and professional advisory services.
Working closely with Federal and State Ministries of Education, regulatory bodies, educational institutions, development partners, and other stakeholders, the Department remains committed to promoting educational excellence, strengthening institutional capacity, enhancing learner outcomes, and advancing inclusive, equitable, and high-quality education in Nigeria.
Our Vision
To make Colleges of Education Centres of Excellence in teaching, learning and research in order to promote the production of top-class teachers, highly motivated and fully prepared for teaching at the basic education level
Our Vision
To be a centre of excellence in educational support services, advancing quality education through professional support, strategic partnerships, and continuous improvement, and contributing to a globally competitive education system in Nigeria.

To provide professional, innovative, and responsive educational support services through effective policy implementation, strategic partnerships, teacher development, evaluation and accreditation of educational qualifications, guidance and counselling, and other evidence-based interventions that promote equitable access to quality education and improved learning outcomes across Nigeria.

Core Functions
Guided by the values of Excellence, Professionalism, Integrity, Innovation, Collaboration, Inclusiveness, Accountability, and Continuous Improvement, the Department’s core functions include:
Educational Qualification Evaluation and Accreditation: evaluates local and foreign educational qualifications, determines Nigerian equivalence where applicable, accredits qualifications outside the statutory mandates of relevant regulatory agencies, and provides advisory services on qualification recognition
National and International Educational Partnerships: coordinates bilateral, multilateral, Commonwealth, and African educational cooperation, facilitates the implementation of education agreements and technical cooperation programmes, and promotes collaboration with regional and international organisations
Teacher Support and Development: strengthens teacher capacity through professional development, instructional support, arts and cultural education, teacher recognition programmes, and initiatives that enhance teaching quality and professional excellence
Guidance, Counselling, and Career Development: develops and promotes guidance and counselling services, career education, psychological assessment, counsellor capacity building, and learner support programmes that foster academic success and personal development
School Health and Sports Education: develops and coordinates programmes that promote school health, physical education, school sports, family life and HIV education, learner well-being, and healthy school environments
Examination Ethics and Campus Safety: coordinates initiatives that promote integrity, transparency, and accountability in public examinations, while strengthening examination ethics, campus safety, and safe learning environments
Instructional Materials, Arts, and Educational Innovation: promotes the development and utilisation of instructional materials, supports arts and cultural education, coordinates educational competitions, and encourages innovative teaching and learning practices
Educational Policy Support, Monitoring, and Stakeholder Collaboration: provides technical and policy advisory services, monitors educational programmes and projects, and collaborates with governments, educational institutions, regulatory agencies, development partners, and other stakeholders to strengthen education service delivery
