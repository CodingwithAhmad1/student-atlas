Alert: Student visa 500 - ASEAN
From 1 July 2026, the visa application charge for Student visas has increased. A lower visa cost applies to eligible Pacific Island and Timor-Leste citizens, eligible ASEAN citizens and applicants in the ELICOS and Non-Award sector who lodge a valid Student visa application. Alert: Visa 500
From 23 March 2024 the Genuine Student requirement replaces the Genuine Temporary Entrant (GTE) requirement for Student visas. Also, applications lodged for a Student or Temporary Graduate visa will be assessed under the New English Language Requirements.
