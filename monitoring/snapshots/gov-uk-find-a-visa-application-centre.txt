Find a visa application centre
Find the opening times, address and contact details for UK visa application centres outside the UK.
If you’re applying from inside the UK, you’ll need to use UK Visa and Citizenship Application Services (UKVCAS) instead.
You must apply for your visa before you can make an appointment at a visa application centre.
If the country you live in does not have a visa application centre, you can choose a centre in a different country.
If you do not choose a visa application centre when you apply for your visa, it will be chosen for you. It will be the centre that’s the most accessible to the country you live in.
