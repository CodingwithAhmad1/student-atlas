Title: Employer Nomination Scheme (subclass 186) visa
Description: For skilled workers who are nominated by an employer to live and work in Australia.
Alert: Subclass 186 (updated 29/07/2026 4:21:48 PM)
Skilled visa income thresholds have increased in line with changes to annual Average Weekly Ordinary Time Earnings. For more information see Annual indexation of skilled visa income thresholds from 1 July 2026.​We prioritise applications in the Employer Nomination Scheme (subclass 186) in accordance with Ministerial Direction No. 119.
