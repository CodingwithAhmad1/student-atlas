Title: Employer Nomination Scheme (subclass 186) visa
Description: For skilled workers who are nominated by an employer to live and work in Australia.
Alert: Subclass 186
Skilled visa income thresholds have increased in line with changes to annual Average Weekly Ordinary Time Earnings. For more information see Annual indexation of skilled visa income thresholds from 1 July 2026.We prioritise visa applications for the Employer Nomination Scheme (subclass 186) visa under Ministerial Direction 122. We are currently processing visa applications that were lodged in:August 2024 – Construction, Healthcare, Teaching, Agriculture, Aquaculture, Fishing, and Resources sectors, or Australia's law enforcement and defence interests.February 2025 – Visa applications from applicants who were in Australia at the time they lodged their application.July 2024 – Visa applications where the primary applicant was outside of Australia at the time they lodged their application and whose application is not combined with another person’s application at anytime.July 2024 – All other visa applications from applicants who were outside Australia at the time they lodged their application.Please note. As we transition to the new priorities under Ministerial Direction 122, processing timeframes may fluctuate. Some published timeframes may temporarily move backwards while applications are reprioritised.
