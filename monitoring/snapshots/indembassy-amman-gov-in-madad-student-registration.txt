Student Registration Module for Indian Students
A ‘Student Registration Module’ is available on MADAD portal (url http://www.madad.gov.in/AppConsular/welcomeLink).The homepage of MADAD Portal provides basic guidance on how students can register themselves on this module. The module is expected to be expanded further with new features and useful information for students.
All Indian students residing in Jordan are urged to register themselves on MADAD portal at the earliest so that they can benefit from student related services provided by the Embassy.
