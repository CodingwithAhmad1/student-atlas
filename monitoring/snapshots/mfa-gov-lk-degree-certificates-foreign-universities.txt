Degree Certificates awarded by foreign Universities
Kindly note: For general instructions on authenticating your documents, please click here.
For the attestation of degree certificates obtained from a foreign University, the original document together with the required number of copies certified correct either by the Sri Lanka embassy in the country concerned or the Embassy of the country concerned established in Sri Lanka should be presented.
However, true copies certified by the British Council in respect of certificates issued by Universities in the United Kingdom and true copies certified by the US Fulbright Commission in respect of certificates issued by Universities in the U.S.A. will be considered for attestation.
