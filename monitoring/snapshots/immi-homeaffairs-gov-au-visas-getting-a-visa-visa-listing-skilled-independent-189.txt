Title: Skilled Independent visa (subclass 189)
Description: This visa is for invited workers and New Zealand citizens with skills we need, to live and work permanently anywhere in Australia.
Alert: 189 visa landing page
The Skilled Independent (subclass 189) (New Zealand stream) visa is currently closed to new applications and will permanently close to new applications on 1 July 2023. For more information see Recent changes.
Alert: 189 visa landing page banner
We prioritise applications for the Skilled – Independent (subclass 189) visa (Points-tested stream) under Ministerial Direction No. 122. We are currently processing applications that were lodged in:November 2025 – Construction, Healthcare, Teaching, Agriculture, Aquaculture, Fishing, and Resources sectors, or in Australia's law enforcement and defence interests.June 2026 – Visa applications from applicants who were in Australia at the time they lodged their application.November 2025 – Visa applications where the primary applicant was outside of Australia at the time they lodged their application and whose application is not combined with another person’s application at any time.November 2025 – All other visa applications from applicants who were outside Australia when they applied.Please note, as we transition to the new priorities under Ministerial Direction No. 122, processing timeframes may fluctuate. Some published timeframes may temporarily move backwards while applications are reprioritised.
Alert: 189 visa permanently closed to new applications on 1 July 2023
The Skilled Independent (subclass 189) (New Zealand stream) visa permanently closed to new applications on 1 July 2023. For more information see Recent changes.
