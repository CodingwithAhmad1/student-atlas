Adopts the policies and guidelines for CAV per CMO 17 s.2013, CMO 5 & 59 s.2016 and Pursuant to CHED COVID-19 Advisory No. 7 dated May 24, 2020 on the Guidelines for the prevention, control and mitigation of the spread of Corona Virus Disease 2019 (COVID-19) in Higher Education Institutions (HEIs) to ensure the safety of the clientele and stakeholders, the following procedure shall be observed.
STEPS AND REQUIREMENTS:
A. Local
Applicant to Private Higher Education Institutions(PHEI)/ Local Universities and Colleges(LUC)
The applicant submits the original and photocopy requirements for the application of CAV;
☐ Diploma (for Graduate)
☐ Official Transcript of Records (TOR)
☐ Certification of the total no. of units earned
(for Unit Earner)
Others (if applicable):
☐ Certification of Correction of Name
☐ Notarized authorization for applicant through a representative with Valid IDs of both parties
Private Higher Education Institution(PHEI)/Local Universities and Colleges(LUC) to CHED Caraga
The Registrar certifies the authenticity of the documents and endorses it to CHED Caraga.
☐ Prescribed Endorsement Form
☐ Diploma (for Graduate)
☐ Official Transcript of Records (TOR)
☐ Certification of the total no. of units earned
(for Unit Earner)
☐ Proof of Payment
Others (if applicable):
☐ Certification of Correction of Name
☐ Notarized authorization for applicant through a representative with Valid IDs of both parties
CHED Caraga
The Records Office validates the completeness of the requirements;
• If complete: Records Office forwards the CAV application to cashier for Official Receipt.
• If payment is confirmed: Cashier issues Official Receipt and forward the same to Records Office.
• Records Office process the CAV.
• If approved: Records Office releases the CAV in Security Paper to PHEI/LUC.
• If with deficiency: Records Office returns the application to PHEI/LUC concerned.
B. Abroad
For CAV application to be used outside the Philippines, follow Steps 1,2 and 3 provided in A. Local.
The applicant brings the CAV in sealed envelope to the preferred DFA Branch/Office.
