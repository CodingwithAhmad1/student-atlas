Temporary Graduate visa
A temporary visa that allows international students to live, study and work after you have finished your studies.
You must: be aged 35 years or under when you apply. *Exceptions apply hold an eligible visa have held a Student visa in the last 6 months have a recent qualification in a CRICOS-registered course apply for your eligible stream (it is not possible to change streams after you apply) attach required evidence when you apply
