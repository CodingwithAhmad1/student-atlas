Description: Visit Australia as often as you wish in a 12-month period. Stay up to 3 months each time you enter Australia.
Alert: ETA-app
Important information about the ETAYou must use the Australian ETA App (the ETA App) to apply for an ETA if you hold an ETA-eligible passport. For more information, see our Step by step guide. If you are unable to use the ETA App, you can apply for another suitable visa through ImmiAccount.European passport holders may be eligible for an eVisitor (subclass 651) visa.Australian permanent residents should see Overseas travel as a permanent resident.Australian citizens are ineligible to apply for an ETA. For more information, see Entering Australia.
