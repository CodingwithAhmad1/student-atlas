A fee of THB 330 applies for reissuing a TB clearance certificate.
The fee for children below 11 years includes a history taking and physical exam. Children may be required to have a chest x-ray or sputum test, which will cost THB 3,800.
The test fee does not cover treatment if you have TB.
What you should bring with you
your original passport
if you are a parent or legal guardian accompanying a child under 18 years of age, bring your original passport or ID card
the address you will stay at in the UK (printed or written down, if available)
Note for students: If you do not know your address exactly, please indicate the university/school that you plan to apply for.
Photo(s) will be taken electronically on the appointment date by IOM staff.
Attending the clinic
You should contact the approved clinic to book an appointment and make it clear that you need the test for a UK visa application. You can expect to get an appointment within a few days of your request.
At busy times you may have to wait 7 days for an appointment, especially during the UK student visa peak season between May and August.
If you need to cancel your appointment, you should contact the clinic as soon as possible.
