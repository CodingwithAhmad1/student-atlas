Title: Work and Holiday visa (subclass 462)
Description: For young adults who want an extended holiday and to work here to fund it
Alert: High volume of WHM visa applications
We are normalising processing timeframes for most WHM visa applications. The average processing time is expected to be around three months. Do not book flights or make travel, work or study commitments until we grant your visa.
For young adults who want an extended holiday and to work here to fund it
Must be 18 to 30 years old (inclusive) Must have a passport from an eligible country Must not be accompanied by dependent children
