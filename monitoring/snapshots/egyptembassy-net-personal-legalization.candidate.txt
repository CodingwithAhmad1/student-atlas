Authentication of Personal Documents
(Employment/Medical/ Educational/ Bringing a Pet to Egypt)
A copy of the original documents is requested for authentication of the documents when applied by mail or in-person
For US Documents such as US Marriage Certificate, US Divorce Decree, Employment Letter, etc…….
The Egyptian Consulate in Washington, D.C. can ONLY legalize documents issued from the States under our jurisdiction ( Delaware, Florida, Georgia, Maryland, N. Carolina, S. Carolina, Virginia, Washington D.C., West Virginia )
For Complete information regarding the jurisdiction of the Egyptian Consulates in the USA, please visit this link: https://egyptembassy.net/consular-services/egyptian-consulate-in-us/
Notary Public Stamp. (If employment letter or medical report, etc.. the Notary Public has to witness the signature of the person in charge / party who wrote the letter and the Notary’s dry seal must be next to the signature not on a separate paper)
Authentication by the Secretary of your Local State (ex. If living in Florida, please send the documents to Tallahassee). Please check the National Association of Secretaries of State website for more information.
Department of State. Any court/marriage (issued from court/county)/Divorce Decree (issued from court/county) must be legalized by the above and department of state before legalized by the Egyptian Consulate.

First step:
· Refer to the website of our Cultural Office to legalize your certificate first and mail them their requirements:

Egyptian Cultural and Educational Bureau – Washington DC – Egyptian Cultural and Educational Bureau- Washington DC – Contact us (eecous.org)
Second Step:
· After legalizing the required document from the Cultural Office, you will need to do final legalization from the closest consulate that serves your state. Kindly choose it from the link here: Egyptian Consulates in US – Embassy of Egypt, Washington DC (egyptembassy.net)
If the consulate in Washington DC serves you, kindly mail or bring the following documents and fees:
– Documents stamped from the Egyptian Cultural & Educational Bureau
– $47 Cash or Cashier’s Check from the bank addressed to consulate of Egypt or Embassy of Egypt for each document (Money orders and personal check are not accepted).
– Pre stamped & pre paid self addressed return envelope with tracking number from USPS (in case you are mailing in your documents).
– Contact information
– We are open Monday to Friday from 9:30 AM to 12:30 PM except for National Holidays. Please bring cash if you will come in person. Kindly always check our Facebook page for holidays and Covid emergency closures. Pick up for documents that requires legalization is at between 3:00 and 4:00 PM
– processing time for mailed in documents is 10 business days
Bringing a pet to Egypt
· A letter from the Veterinarian that the pet meets the healthy requirements and it is clear from diseases.
· To be authenticated by the USDA – U.S. Department of Agriculture’s Animal and Plant Health Inspection Service (APHIS)
· please note that it must have the dry embossed seal for the Agriculture in section service for your document to be legalized
· The USDA certificate is only valid for 14 days once it is issued, so plan accordingly and take in consideration our processing time as well
· Copy of the owner`s passport.
· Fees are $132 cash or cashier’s check from the bank (kindly note that money orders and personal checks are not accepted)
· If you come in person, you can visit within our business hours from 9:30 AM to 12:30 PM, Monday to Friday, and pick is between 3:00 and 4:00 PM same day.
· You can legalize the documents by mail as well, kindly include the following in addition to the above requirements:
· If you mail the document, include a return envelope from USPS that is paid, self-addressed, and with a tracking number (express is strongly advised)
· Mailed in documents take at least 10 business days to be processed
· $132 cashier’s check from the bank addressed to the Egyptian consulate
· Note with your contact information
The Consulate accepts Walk-ins from Monday through Friday from 9:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. Pick up is between 3:00 and 4:00 PM.

MAILED DOCUMENTS: Please provide only Express Mail or Certified Mail (pre-addressed, pre-stamped envelope for the return of the documents). The Consulate will not be held responsible for any loss of documents by mail.
Important Mailing Instructions
We only accept mailers from the United States Postal Service (USPS). FedEx, UPS, and other carriers are not accepted.
Please ensure your documents are sent in a USPS Priority Mail envelope (approximately 11″ x 8.5″).
Submissions received in unapproved packaging will remain pending and unprocessed until the correct envelope is provided.
Update Your Bank Account Information in Egypt (NBE / Banque Misr)
To update your bank account information with National Bank of Egypt (NBE) or Banque Misr, please follow these simple steps:
Complete the Data Update Form and bring it with you (Download file below).
Visit the Consular Section in person.
Present one original valid Egyptian identification document:
Egyptian National ID Card (valid), or
Egyptian Passport (valid).
Bring a clear color copy of the identification document presented (color copy preferred).
Sign the form before the consular officer.
Pay the signature authentication fee of $47.00 cash.
The bank will process your request and contact you directly if any additional documents or actions are required.
Important Notes
You do not need a notification from the bank to use this service.
The service is available to all customers of National Bank of Egypt and Banque Misr.
Please review the bank’s requirements and conditions before your visit.
