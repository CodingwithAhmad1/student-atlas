What does CEMS do?
CEMS enables GTEC to evaluate educational
credentials such as certificates, diplomas
and degrees obtained locally or abroad and
provide an evaluation report indicating their
equivalence.
About Credential Evaluation
GTEC provides credential evaluation services
to help employers, educational institutions
and other organizations understand foreign
and local educational qualifications.
Educational Qualifications
Certificates, diplomas and degrees
submitted for evaluation are assessed
based on available information about
the qualification and awarding institution.
Ghanaian Equivalence
Evaluation reports assist users in
understanding the equivalence of a
qualification within the Ghanaian
education system.
Evaluation Reports
Completed evaluations produce reports
that can assist employers and educational
institutions in making informed decisions.

How the Service Works
The general stages involved in processing
a credential evaluation request.
1
Apply
Submit your credential evaluation
application through CEMS.
2
Payment
Pay the applicable evaluation
processing fee.
3
Verification
Submitted credentials may be
verified with the relevant institution.
4
Evaluation
GTEC assesses the qualification and
prepares the evaluation report.
Important Guidelines
Please review these requirements before
submitting an application.
What CEMS Does Not Evaluate
Result slips and letters of attestation.
Certificates of participation or
seminar/workshop attendance.
Courses from incomplete programmes.
Individual course grades or workplace
ranking.
Documents and Translation
Official academic records should be
submitted.
Non-English documents must be accompanied
by certified English translations.
Relevant certificates must accompany
an evaluation request.
GTEC reserves the right to verify
submitted documents.
Change of Name or Address
Certificates bearing different names
should be accompanied by relevant
supporting documents.
Requests to change a mailing address
should be submitted in writing.
Processing and Possible Delays
Evaluation is not an instant service.
Verification responses may affect
processing time.
Additional research or information
may occasionally be required.
Applicants should submit requests
well ahead of required deadlines.
