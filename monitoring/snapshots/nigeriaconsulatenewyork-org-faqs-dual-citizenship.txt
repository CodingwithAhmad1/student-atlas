Frequently Asked Questions
Welcome to the Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) page of the Consulate General of Nigeria in New York. This section is designed to provide clear and concise answers to the most common enquiries we receive about our consular services, including passport issuance and renewal, visa applications, document authentication, emergency travel certificates, and more. Whether you are a Nigerian citizen, a foreign national seeking entry to Nigeria, or a member of the general public, the resources on this page and our website will help guide you through key processes and requirements.

General Questions about the Consulate General of Nigeria in New York
The Consulate General of Nigeria in New York is open from Monday through Friday. It is closed on all Nigerian and U.S. Federal Public holidays. Please check our Official Holidays page to confirm if the Consulate is open before visiting.

Most people interested in visiting Nigeria require an Entry Visa. However, Nigerians who hold expired visas may be exempt from this requirement. In addition, Citizens of ECOWAS Countries do not require Visas to enter the Country if they are staying for less than 90 days.
