Resolution No. (5) of 2025
Regarding the Standards for UAE Nationals Studying Abroad in Higher Education
Institutions
The Chairman of the Education, Human Development, and Community Development
Council,
- Having reviewed Federal Decree-Law No. (28) of 2024, amending certain provisions
of Federal Law No. (1) of 1972 on the competencies of the Ministries and Powers of
the Ministers;
- Federal Decree-Law No. (48) of 2021 On Higher Education;
- Federal Decree-Law No. (42) of 2024 concerning the Establishment and Regulation of
the Education, Human Development, and Community Development Council;
- Federal Decree-Law No. (49) of 2022 on Human Resources in the Federal Government;
- Cabinet Resolution No. (48) of 2023 concerning the Executive Regulation of Federal
Decree-Law No. (49) of 2022 Concerning Human Resources in the Federal
Government;
- The Resolution of the Minister of Higher Education and Scientific Research No. (93)
of 2010 concerning the Conditions for Equivalence of Certificates for Students
Studying Abroad;
- The Resolution of the Minister of Higher Education and Scientific Research No. (34)
of 2025 regarding the recognition of qualifications issued by foreign higher education
institutions (HEIs); and
- Based on the proposal of the Minister of Higher Education and Scientific Research and
the requirements of public interest,
Hereby resolve as follows:
Article (1) Definitions
For the purposes of this Resolution, the following words and expressions shall have the
meanings assigned thereto respectively unless the context requires otherwise:
The State : The United Arab Emirates.
The Minister : The Minister of Higher Education and Scientific Research.
The Ministry : The Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research.
The Council : The Education, Human Development, and Community
Development Council.
Emirati Student : A UAE national who is studying abroad, whether on
scholarships or self-funded, for the purpose of obtaining an
academic or professional qualification.
Scholarship
Provider
: The entity responsible for sending the Emirati Student abroad
and covering their study expenses.
Article (2) Objectives
These standards aim to regulate the higher education studies of Emirati Students abroad
and control the use of qualifications obtained thereby for the purposes of employment,
promotions, study leave, examination leave, research thesis defense leave, or qualification-
related benefits in the federal government sector.
Article (3) Obligations of the Emirati Student and Scholarship Providers
First: An Emirati Student desiring to enroll in a higher education institution outside the
State, whether at their own expense or at the expense of a Scholarship Provider, shall be
obligated to pursue studies at a higher education institution that meets one of the following
conditions:
a. Must be ranked among the world’s top 50 universities for the targeted specialisation,
regardless of the general classification or country of study; according to the global
rankings accredited by the Ministry; or
b. Must be ranked among the world’s top 100 universities for the targeted
specialisation, and among the top 100 universities of the general classification in
case of studying in the USA, Australia, or the UK; or
c. Must be ranked among the world’s top 200 universities for the targeted
specialisation, and among the top 200 universities of the general classification in
case of studying in an English-speaking country other than the USA, Australia, or
the UK; or
d. Must be ranked among the world’s top 300 universities for the targeted
specialisation, and among the top 300 universities of the general classification in
case of studying in a non-English-speaking country.
Second: The Council may exclude any higher education institutions that meet the
conditions for studying outside the State, based on reasons and justifications deemed
appropriate thereby.
Article (4) Violation of the Resolution
Failure by the Emirati Student or the Scholarship Provider to comply with the provisions
of this Resolution will result in the non-recognition of the qualification obtained by the
Emirati Student. Furthermore, this qualification will not be considered for purposes of
employment, promotion, study leave, examination leave, thesis discussion leave, or
qualification-related benefits in the federal government sector in the State.
Article (5) Adjustment of Status
Emirati Student currently studying abroad, whether self-funded or sponsored, are granted
a one-year grace period from the effective date of this Resolution to adjust their academic
status.
Article (6) Exceptions
A committee shall be formed by a resolution of the Chairman of the Education, Human
Development, and Community Development Council. This committee shall include
representatives from selected scholarship providers within the State. The resolution shall
specify the committee's modus operandi. The committee shall be responsible for
considering cases warranting exception from the provisions of this Resolution, based on
specific considerations related to scholarship provider, the host country, the field of study,
personal circumstances, or other justifiable reasons. The committee shall submit its
recommendation, including the reasons and justifications, to the Chairman of the Council,
who shall then issue a resolution regarding the granting or denial of the exception.
Article (7) Publication and Entry into Force
This Resolution shall be published in the Official Gazette and shall enter into force (3) three
months following its publishing date.
