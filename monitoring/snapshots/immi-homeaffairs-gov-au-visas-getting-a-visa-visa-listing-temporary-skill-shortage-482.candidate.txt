Title: Skills in Demand visa (subclass 482)
Description: This temporary visa lets an employer sponsor a suitably skilled worker to fill a position they can’t find a suitably skilled Australian to fill.
Skills in Demand visa
This temporary visa lets an employer sponsor a suitably skilled worker to fill a position they can’t find a suitably skilled Australian to fill.
You must: Have an approved sponsor and be nominated for the visa
