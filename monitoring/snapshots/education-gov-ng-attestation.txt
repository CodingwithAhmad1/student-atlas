FEDERAL MINISTRY OF EDUCATION EVALUATION AND ACCREDITATION DIVISION DEPARTMENT OF EDUCATION SUPPORT SERVICE
ACTIVITIES OF EVALUATION AND ACCREDITATION DIVISION
The evaluation & accreditation division of the education support services department performs the following activities.
Evaluation of both Local Foreign Qualifications for employment and admission purposes.
Authentication of academic and professional qualifications awarded in Nigeria for study abroad.
Issuing of eligibility letters for candidates wishing to travel abroad for studies and employments purposes.
Issuing of no objection letter to institutes or other educational outfits.
REQUIREMENTS
1. EVALUATION
Degree certificate
Transcript
O’Level certificates (5 credits including English & Mathematics in at least 2 sittings)
International Passport
Admission letter
Amount to be paid; ₦500.00 only
2. AUTHENTICATON
SSCE result issued by WAEC, NECO, NABTEB & NBAIS
O’Level certificates (ND, HND, DEGREE, MASTERS & Ph.D)
Transcript
ID card (for professionals such as Nursing, Doctors, Medical Laboratory)
Amount to be paid; 300.00 per page
3. ELIGIBILITY LETTERS
The candidate must authenticate the certificate first.
Eligibility for undergraduate programmes: candidates must have 5 credits including English mathematics in at least 2 sittings
Eligibility for Masters programmes: candidates must possess bachelor’s degree from Nigerian tertiary institutions,
Eligibility for Ph.D programmes: candidates must possess master’s degree from Nigerian tertiary institution.
Amount to be paid is 500.00 per letter.
