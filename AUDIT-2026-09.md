# Audit of the existing nationality overlays (Phase 0 of `EXPANSION-PLAN.md`)

_2026-09-27. Read-only classification of every hand-written callout in
`frontend/src/data/nationalities/*.ts`, to decide what survives the migration
to the trait layer. Nothing has been edited yet; Phase 1 step 5 applies this._

Verdicts: **KEEP+CITE** (stable official fact, add a citation),
**TRAIT** (the claim is a list membership, replaced by a generated callout),
**PRACTICAL** (uncited advice, allowed under D2, no numbers),
**DELETE** (volatile, unverifiable, stale, currency conversion, or duplicate).
"VERIFY" marks claims believed stale or wrong.

## Systemic findings

1. **Volatile numbers everywhere.** Consulate wait times, processing weeks,
   "peak season" months and refusal-rate commentary appear in every file and
   account for most deletions. The plan's rule (no numbers without a
   citation; wait times link to the official tool) removes the whole class.
2. **Stale Australian terminology.** GTE (replaced by the Genuine Student
   requirement, 2024) and "assessment levels" (replaced by evidence levels,
   2024) appear in UAE, Pakistan, India and China. Any surviving AU callout
   must be re-checked against immi.homeaffairs.gov.au before it ships.
3. **Currency conversions** (AED, PKR, INR, CNY) of the £41,700 Skilled
   Worker threshold appear in four files. All deleted.
4. **Duplication.** The US visa-bulletin story is told two or three times per
   file (post-study, sponsorship, residency); the AU "points test is
   nationality-blind" line likewise. The R1 trait renders it once.
5. **Non-government sources.** VFS Global centre lists were proposed as
   citations; VFS is a commercial partner, so those callouts become
   PRACTICAL (no city list) rather than KEEP+CITE (override of the China
   audit row 15).
6. **Dual nationality** is already the pattern the plan wants: India cites
   ociservices.gov.in and UAE cites uaecabinet.ae. Pakistan's "bilateral
   agreements" wording reflects the Pakistan Citizenship Act section 14
   notified-country list and needs a Pakistani government citation.
